# ITPM-project-
The E-Channeling System lets patients book doctors, pay online, and get AI help to find the right specialist. Doctors manage appointments and view earnings. Admins handle doctor profiles and cancellations. Payment managers update status, and blog writers share health tips.


E-Channeling System Description
The E-Channeling System is an online platform that simplifies healthcare appointment management. It supports multiple user roles with specific functionalities to ensure a smooth and efficient experience.

Patients can book appointments with doctors based on availability and specialization. Payments are made securely through the online payment system. If a patient is unsure about which doctor to choose, an AI assistant is available to suggest the most suitable specialist based on symptoms.

Doctors can manage their appointments with options to cancel or mark them as completed. They can also view total earnings and track the number of appointments they’ve received.

Admins oversee the platform and are responsible for adding or removing doctors and canceling appointments when needed.

Payment Managers handle financial operations by updating payment statuses as either “Completed” or “Failed” based on the result of transactions.

Blog Writers can add health-related blogs to the system. These blogs are accessible to all users and provide valuable information on healthcare and wellness topics.

The system is designed to offer a secure, user-friendly experience for both patients and healthcare professionals. By integrating smart tools like AI assistance and role-based access, it improves the overall efficiency and accessibility of healthcare services.
